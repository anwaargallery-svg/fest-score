About Anwariyya

Empowering minds with knowledge and moral values since 1969.

Anwariyya Arabic College is a premier institution dedicated to the profound study of Arabic language and Islamic sciences. Established in 1969, the college has a rich history of nurturing students and guiding them towards academic excellence and spiritual depth. We offer comprehensive programs such as Hifz, Shareeath, and Muthawal, preparing our graduates (Anwarees) to be knowledgeable and responsible leaders in society.
